## Quantum Gravity & Spacetime Foam

### Ng & Perlman (2022) - Spacetime Foam Review
- **File**: [2205.12852v3.pdf](refs/2205.12852v3.pdf)
- **Title**: Probing Spacetime Foam with Extragalactic Sources of High-Energy Photons
- **Authors**: Y. Jack Ng, Eric S. Perlman
- **Publication**: Universe 8(7):382
- **Year**: 2022
- **DOI**: [10.3390/universe8070382](https://doi.org/10.3390/universe8070382)
- **Summary**: Review of observational methods to constrain quantum foam models using high-energy photons from distant sources. Discusses detecting wave-front distortions from spacetime foam using quasars, active galactic nuclei, and interferometry. Covers angular broadening, phase incoherence accumulation, and fringe visibility reduction at cosmological baselines.
- **Keywords**: spacetime foam, quantum gravity, high-energy photons, GRB, quasars, interferometry, phase coherence, wave-front distortion

### Amelino-Camelia (2013) - Quantum Spacetime Phenomenology
- **File**: [0806.0339v2.pdf](refs/0806.0339v2.pdf)
- **Title**: Quantum-Spacetime Phenomenology
- **Author**: Giovanni Amelino-Camelia
- **Publication**: Living Reviews in Relativity 16:5
- **Year**: 2013
- **arXiv**: [0806.0339](https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.0339)
- **DOI**: [10.12942/lrr-2013-5](https://doi.org/10.12942/lrr-2013-5)
- **Summary**: Comprehensive review of phenomenological programs testing quantum-spacetime theories. Emphasizes data analyses sensitive to Planck-scale effects and phenomenological approaches that influenced quantum gravity theory development.
- **Keywords**: quantum spacetime, Planck scale, phenomenology, quantum gravity, observational tests

### Lieu & Hillman (2003) - Phase Coherence & Planck-Scale Fluctuations
- **File**: [Lieu_2003_ApJ_585_L77.pdf](refs/Lieu_2003_ApJ_585_L77.pdf)
- **Title**: The Phase Coherence of Light from Extragalactic Sources: Direct Evidence against First-Order Planck-Scale Fluctuations in Time and Space
- **Authors**: Richard Lieu, Lloyd W. Hillman
- **Publication**: The Astrophysical Journal 585:L77–L80
- **Year**: 2003
- **DOI**: [10.1086/374350](https://doi.org/10.1086/374350)
- **Summary**: Presents observational test of quantum gravity's prediction that spacetime has ultimate uncertainties at the Planck scale. Uses phase coherence of light from distant point sources to constrain Planck-scale fluctuations. Argues accumulated phase incoherence would destroy coherence over cosmological distances.
- **Keywords**: quantum gravity, Planck scale, phase coherence, extragalactic sources, spacetime fluctuations

## CMB & Cosmological Observations

### Chluba et al. (2021) - CMB Spectral Distortions (Voyage 2050)
- **File**: [s10686-021-09729-5.pdf](refs/s10686-021-09729-5.pdf)
- **Title**: New Horizons in Cosmology with Spectral Distortions of the Cosmic Microwave Background
- **Lead Author**: Jens Chluba (and 30+ authors)
- **Publication**: Experimental Astronomy 51:1515–1554
- **Year**: 2021
- **DOI**: [10.1007/s10686-021-09729-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10686-021-09729-5)
- **Summary**: Voyage 2050 paper describing science opportunities with CMB spectral distortions. Covers processes from inflation to structure formation. Discusses precision spectroscopy for testing cosmological standard model and discovering new physics including dark matter and particle physics constraints.
- **Keywords**: CMB spectral distortions, inflation, recombination, reionization, structure formation, spectroscopy, early universe

### Kogut et al. (2019) - CMB Spectral Distortions (Astro2020)
- **File**: [1907.13195v1.pdf](refs/1907.13195v1.pdf)
- **Title**: CMB Spectral Distortions: Status and Prospects
- **Lead Author**: A. Kogut (and 7 co-authors)
- **Publication**: Astro2020 Decadal Survey APC White Paper
- **Year**: 2019
- **arXiv**: [1907.07558](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.07558)
- **Summary**: Astro2020 white paper on CMB spectral distortions. Reviews current status and future prospects for detecting and measuring distortions from early universe physics. Complements CMB anisotropy studies with spectroscopic information.
- **Keywords**: CMB spectral distortions, Astro2020, early universe, cosmology, missions

### Tagliazucchi et al. (2025) - CMB Distortions & Pulsar Timing Arrays
- **File**: [PhysRevD.111.L021305.pdf](refs/PhysRevD.111.L021305.pdf)
- **Title**: Quest for CMB Spectral Distortions to Probe the Scalar-Induced Gravitational Wave Background Interpretation of Pulsar Timing Array Data
- **Authors**: Matteo Tagliazucchi, Matteo Braglia, Fabio Finelli, Mauro Pieroni
- **Publication**: Physical Review D 111:L021305
- **Year**: 2025
- **DOI**: [10.1103/PhysRevD.111.L021305](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.111.L021305)
- **Summary**: Studies how CMB spectral distortions and anisotropies constrain scalar-induced gravitational wave interpretations of recent pulsar timing array measurements. Shows future PIXIE-like experiments can detect CMB spectral distortions from scalar-induced SGWB.
- **Keywords**: CMB spectral distortions, gravitational waves, pulsar timing arrays, primordial fluctuations, PIXIE

### Fixsen et al. (1996) - COBE/FIRAS CMB Spectrum
- **File**: [9605054v1.pdf](refs/9605054v1.pdf)
- **Title**: The Cosmic Microwave Background Spectrum from the Full COBE FIRAS Data Set
- **Authors**: D. J. Fixsen, E. S. Cheng, J. M. Gales, J. C. Mather, R. A. Shafer, E. L. Wright
- **Publication**: The Astrophysical Journal 473:576–587
- **Year**: 1996
- **arXiv**: [astro-ph/9605054](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9605054)

## Gamma-Ray Bursts & High-Energy Astrophysics

### Ravasio et al. (2024) - GRB 221009A MeV Emission Line
- **File**: [science.adj3638.pdf](refs/science.adj3638.pdf)
- **Title**: A Mega-Electron Volt Emission Line in the Spectrum of a Gamma-Ray Burst
- **Lead Author**: Maria Edvige Ravasio (and co-authors)
- **Publication**: Science 385:452–455
- **Year**: 2024
- **DOI**: [10.1126/science.adj3638](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adj3638)
- **Summary**: Reports detection of narrow emission feature at ~10 MeV in the spectrum of GRB 221009A, the brightest gamma-ray burst ever observed. Features narrow (~1 MeV width) emission that evolves in energy (12–6 MeV) and luminosity over 80 seconds.
- **Keywords**: GRB 221009A, gamma-ray bursts, MeV emission line, spectral features, high-energy astrophysics

### Zhang et al. (2024) - GRB 221009A Spectral Lines
- **File**: [2403.12851v2.pdf](refs/2403.12851v2.pdf)
- **Title**: Observation of Spectral Lines in the Exceptional GRB 221009A
- **Lead Author**: Yan-Qiu Zhang (and 21 co-authors)
- **Publication**: Science China Physics, Mechanics & Astronomy
- **Year**: 2024
- **arXiv**: [2403.12851](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12851)
- **Summary**: Comprehensive spectroscopy analysis of GRB 221009A exploring spectral line features. Complementary analysis to Ravasio et al. study of the same remarkable event, the brightest gamma-ray burst ever observed.

## AGN & Quasars

### Leighly et al. (2007) - X-Ray Weak Quasar PHL 1811
- **File**: [0705.0940v1.pdf](refs/0705.0940v1.pdf)
- **Title**: The Intrinsically X-Ray Weak Quasar PHL 1811. II. Optical and UV Spectra and Analysis
- **Authors**: Karen M. Leighly, Jules P. Halpern, Edward B. Jenkins, Darrin Casebeer
- **Publication**: The Astrophysical Journal Supplements 173:1–36
- **Year**: 2007
- **arXiv**: [0705.0940](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.0940)
- **DOI**: [10.1086/519768](https://doi.org/10.1086/519768)
- **Summary**: Second in a series on unusually X-ray weak quasar PHL 1811. Reports HST STIS optical and UV spectra plus ground-based optical data. Shows unusual optical/UV line emission with strong Fe II/Fe III and low-ionization lines (Na I, Ca II) but no forbidden lines.
- **Keywords**: quasars, AGN, X-ray weak, spectroscopy, optical, UV, iron emission

### Feruglio et al. (2011) - Compton-Thick AGN
- **File**: [1101.3478v1.pdf](refs/1101.3478v1.pdf)
- **Title**: Discovery of Strong Iron K-Alpha Emitting Compton-Thick Quasars at z=2.5 and 2.9
- **Authors**: C. Feruglio, E. Daddi, F. Fiore, D. M. Alexander, E. Piconcelli, C. Malacaria
- **Publication**: The Astrophysical Journal Letters 729:L4
- **Year**: 2011
- **arXiv**: [1101.3478](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.3478)
- **DOI**: [10.1088/2041-8205/729/1/L4](https://doi.org/10.1088/2041-8205/729/1/L4)
- **Summary**: Detection of 6.4 keV iron K-alpha emission in two massive, star-forming galaxies at z~2.5-2.9 hosting Compton-thick obscured AGN. Demonstrates how iron K-alpha lines probe heavily obscured AGN and estimate intrinsic luminosities much larger than observed values.
- **Keywords**: AGN, quasars, Compton-thick, iron K-alpha, X-ray, obscuration, high-redshift
